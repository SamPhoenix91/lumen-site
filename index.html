What Lumen is

Plex is the default and drives Home. Jellyfin and Emby servers sit alongside it, and IPTV adds live channels and an on-demand library you browse yourself. Everything plays through Apple's own player, with AetherEngine stepping in for formats Apple's player can't open.

Your servers

Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and IPTV, merged into one place.

Private

Lumen talks only to your servers, iCloud and your own devices, with a title-only lookup for subtitles and skip times.

Apple's player

Native playback with the controls you know, and AetherEngine as a fallback for everything else.

Every screen

One app, the same layout and settings on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad.

Privacy

Lumen has no accounts of its own, no analytics and no tracking. The short version is below; the full privacy policy has the details.

  • Sign-ins stay with you. Server and provider sign-ins are kept in your device's Keychain. With iCloud Sync on, they're also stored in CloudKit's end-to-end encrypted fields, and nowhere else.
  • No analytics, no tracking. Lumen doesn't collect usage data and has no accounts of its own.
  • Only your servers. Your media and sign-ins go only to the servers and providers you add.
  • Lookups know the title, not you. To find subtitles and intro timings, Lumen sends only the title's IMDb ID (with season and episode numbers for a TV episode). It never sends your account, sign-ins, file names or library.
  • Encrypted between your devices. Everything Local Network Sync sends, including Play On and Watch Together, is encrypted with AES-256 using a key only your paired devices hold.
  • Your watch history stays yours. Positions are saved on your device and sent back only to your own Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server.
  • Device name. Shown only to you, in the list of your own devices (Settings > Sync) and in the player's Play On and Watch Together menus. It's sent only over your local network, encrypted and directly between your own devices, and never to any server, cloud service, analytics or third party.
